Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

CipherNest does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal information. Your data stays on your device, encrypted, and under your control.

CipherNest ("we", "us", or "our") operates the CipherNest mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our Service.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal information.

CipherNest is designed with privacy at its core. All your data — including passwords, secure notes, bank cards, identification documents (ID cards, passports), and any other sensitive information you choose to store — remains encrypted and stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to your data, and we never will.

2. How We Store Your Data

Your vault is protected with military-grade encryption and stored exclusively on your device. Your master password never leaves your device and is never sent to any server. Without your master password, no one — including us — can access your data.

We do not use cloud storage, we do not create backups on our servers, and we do not have any mechanism to retrieve your data if you lose your master password.

3. Data Security

We are committed to protecting your data. The app uses industry-standard strong encryption to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

CipherNest does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not share your data with anyone.

The app may use system-provided features (such as biometric authentication via Face ID or Touch ID) that are handled by your device's operating system. These features are governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

5. In-App Purchases

CipherNest offers in-app purchases (subscription and lifetime purchase). Payment processing is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Please refer to Apple's privacy policy for details on how your payment data is handled.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
